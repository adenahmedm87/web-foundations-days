About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing ideas and reminders. It is designed to help you keep your notes together so you can return to them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea in the New note field.
  3. Use Add Note to submit your note once this feature is implemented.
  4. Review your notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • A clearly labelled field for writing a note.
  • A list showing sample notes.
  • A note count showing the number of sample notes.
  • Navigation between the Home and About pages.

This version is an HTML skeleton. Adding notes will be implemented later.
